# Future Companion Collection Ideas

This is the living backlog for ordinary companion collections in Number Nook. It keeps promising themes in one place before individual rosters, permanent IDs, rarity assignments, or artwork are approved. New ideas can remain brief here until one is selected for a complete collection document.

The live catalog contains the ten Nook Neighbors, ten Nookside Pups, ten dual-art Lantern Lane Cats, and Button Bunny as a Special Guest. Future ordinary collections should continue using the established four Common, three Uncommon, two Rare, and one Legendary distribution unless acquisition data justifies a change.

## Selection principles

A strong collection should:

- give ten companions visibly different silhouettes, palettes, personalities, and signature details;
- have a shared place or story rather than relying only on species;
- remain recognizable in locked silhouettes and 96-pixel portraits;
- support both Simple SVG and Polished Sticker art without excessive costume detail;
- provide useful themes, motifs, dialogue voices, and collection-completion moments;
- feel exciting at every rarity rather than treating Common companions as filler;
- broaden the catalog without making the application depend on species-specific logic;
- be practical to describe, generate, review, and maintain as a consistent batch.

Cats should remain the catalog's center of gravity for now, but later collections can freely feature other species or a deliberate mixture.

## Candidate collections

### Lantern Lane Cats — Selected

Ten cats who come alive along a cozy little lane from late afternoon through starlight: a café-window cat, a lamplighter, a rooftop watcher, a tiny florist, a raincoat explorer, and other evening neighbors.

- **Why it works:** Reaches the thirty-one-companion target while keeping cats in the majority. Warm windows, lanterns, rain reflections, dusk skies, and shopfronts create a strong identity distinct from the broader Nook Neighbors.
- **Art opportunities:** Excellent palettes and lighting, recognizable accessories, cozy backgrounds, and natural Common-to-Legendary escalation.
- **Watch-outs:** Avoid making every portrait dark, purple, or nocturnal. The cats still need varied daytime-readable silhouettes and colors.
- **Status:** Selected and fully implemented as a ten-member dual-art collection across catalogs 2.5 and 2.6. The complete roster, approved Sticker sources, and art contract live in [The Lantern Lane Cats Collection](./LANTERN_LANE_CATS.md).

### Harbor Whiskers

Ten seaside cats who live around a gentle storybook harbor: a shell collector, a lighthouse keeper, a sailmaker, a tide-pool observer, and a legendary stargazing navigator.

- **Why it works:** Another cat collection with a very different visual world and many clear occupations.
- **Art opportunities:** Sea-glass colors, striped fabrics, ropes, maps, shells, docks, boats, and lighthouse glow.
- **Watch-outs:** Keep nautical props restrained and avoid making every cat wear a sailor costume.

### Garden Winglets

Ten small birds who share a bright community garden, with personalities inspired by song, seeds, weather, flowers, and flight.

- **Why it works:** Birds create the clearest silhouette change from cats and dogs while remaining cute, colorful, and family-friendly.
- **Art opportunities:** Dramatically different beaks, crests, tails, wing shapes, perches, flowers, and seasonal palettes.
- **Watch-outs:** The portrait framing and companion dialogue layout must make small bodies feel as substantial as cat and dog portraits.

### Meadow Bunnies

Ten living rabbit companions from a patchwork meadow community: gardeners, bakers, messengers, cloud-watchers, and festival planners.

- **Why it works:** Naturally cute, easy to read at small sizes, and compatible with the game's cozy tone without duplicating Button Bunny's plush identity.
- **Art opportunities:** Ear poses create strong silhouettes; meadow flowers, burrows, baskets, and patchwork colors provide cohesion.
- **Watch-outs:** Keep them visibly living rabbits so Button Bunny remains a distinctive plush Special Guest.

### Whistlewood Wanderers

Ten woodland neighbors of mixed species, such as a fox, deer, raccoon, squirrel, hedgehog, bear, otter, mole, skunk, and owl.

- **Why it works:** Maximum variety and a strong “who will I discover?” feeling within one collection.
- **Art opportunities:** Every portrait can have a unique silhouette, scale, palette, habitat, and personality.
- **Watch-outs:** Harder to make visually cohesive and significantly more demanding for Simple SVG templates, art prompts, dialogue voices, and comparative review.

### Pocket Dragons

Ten friendly miniature dragons whose harmless talents involve sparks, mist, flowers, bubbles, music, warm bread, or glowing stars rather than combat.

- **Why it works:** High fantasy appeal, strong rarity escalation, and unusually memorable Legendary possibilities.
- **Art opportunities:** Wings, horns, tails, scales, elemental palettes, magical motifs, and expressive poses.
- **Watch-outs:** Anatomy consistency is harder for generated art. Designs must remain gentle, uncluttered, and clearly distinct without adding too many horns or effects.

### Tiny Time Dinosaurs

Ten cheerful young dinosaurs from a soft prehistoric valley, each based on a clearly different body shape and curiosity.

- **Why it works:** Instantly recognizable silhouettes and broad kid appeal; unlike anything currently in the catalog.
- **Art opportunities:** Crests, plates, long necks, horns, feathers, fern-filled settings, eggshell motifs, and volcanic sunset colors.
- **Watch-outs:** Generated anatomy may drift, and scientific detail should never overwhelm the friendly storybook identity.

### Pondside Pals

Ten companions from one lively pond, potentially including frogs, ducks, turtles, salamanders, fish, dragonflies, and an otter.

- **Why it works:** A playful mixed-species collection with a clear home and plenty of color.
- **Art opportunities:** Lily pads, reeds, reflections, rain, bubbles, ripples, shells, and water plants.
- **Watch-outs:** Aquatic and flying characters need portrait compositions that still feel like equippable companions rather than scenery.

### Toybox Friends

Ten original toys with different materials and histories: plush animals, a wooden pull toy, a cloth doll, a tin robot, a sock creature, and other repaired favorites.

- **Why it works:** Extends Button Bunny's emotional appeal and makes unusual species or shapes feel natural in one collection.
- **Art opportunities:** Stitches, buttons, patches, wood grain, painted metal, fabric patterns, and a cozy playroom world.
- **Watch-outs:** Keep every design original, warm, and companion-like. Mixed materials make visual consistency more difficult.

### Nook Workshop Robots

Ten friendly little robots built for creative, domestic, or exploratory jobs rather than fighting.

- **Why it works:** Broadens the game beyond animals and creates very clear silhouettes and signature motions.
- **Art opportunities:** Screens, lights, springs, wheels, antennae, tool shapes, retro colors, and clever CSS motifs.
- **Watch-outs:** Metal designs can feel cold or generic; faces and shapes must remain expressive, soft, and handmade.

### Orchard Friends

Ten farm and orchard animals who help with harvests, picnics, weather watching, and seasonal celebrations.

- **Why it works:** Familiar animals, clear roles, warm colors, and easy-to-understand personalities.
- **Art opportunities:** Barns, baskets, blossoms, apples, hay, quilts, windmills, and autumn light.
- **Watch-outs:** Avoid generic clip-art farm animals and repetitive red-barn backgrounds.

### Starry Familiars

Ten gentle magical creatures connected by a small observatory or spellbook library: winged kittens, moon moths, tiny griffins, cloud foxes, and other original fantasy companions.

- **Why it works:** Offers the most freedom and can support especially desirable Rare and Legendary companions.
- **Art opportunities:** Constellations, glowing markings, unusual silhouettes, floating objects, and rich companion themes.
- **Watch-outs:** The concept can become visually noisy or inconsistent quickly. It needs a stricter shape language and effect budget than an ordinary animal collection.

## Special Guest backlog

Special Guests are individual exceptions rather than substitutes for ordinary collections. Suitable future directions include:

- an original requested plush or comfort character;
- a one-off holiday visitor;
- a family-designed imaginary creature;
- a character that celebrates completing a collection or major goal;
- an unusual cat, dog, bird, or other species that intentionally sits outside a collection's story.

Special Guests should remain rare, use original or appropriately authorized artwork, and normally arrive beside enough ordinary content that they do not distort capsule variety.

## Idea template

Add future ideas using this lightweight format:

```md
### Collection name

One-sentence shared-world pitch.

- **Why it works:** Player and product value.
- **Art opportunities:** Distinctive silhouettes, colors, props, and settings.
- **Watch-outs:** Consistency, complexity, overlap, or production risks.
```
