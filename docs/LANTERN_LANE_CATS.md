# The Lantern Lane Cats Collection

Lantern Lane Cats is Number Nook's third ordinary collection and second cat collection. It reaches the thirty-one-companion kid-ready target while giving the catalog a visually focused evening neighborhood distinct from the broad, home-centered Nook Neighbors and the outdoor Nookside Pups.

## Shared world

Lantern Lane bends through a cozy row of small shops, upstairs windows, garden gates, roofs, and lampposts. The collection moves from late-afternoon café warmth through rain-washed blue hour to Lantern Night. It should feel active and welcoming, never dark or sleepy.

The cats belong together because they share the lane and help it glow in different ways. They do not wear a uniform. Their professions and props remain light character signals rather than costumes.

Shared visual ingredients include:

- warm windows against cooler street colors;
- lanterns, reflections, awnings, shop signs without lettering, rooflines, and small flower boxes;
- controlled amber highlights balanced by teal, blue, plum, green, coral, and silver palettes;
- one readable signature object or marking per cat;
- backgrounds simple enough to survive both a 96-pixel view and the locked grayscale treatment.

Avoid ten dark-purple portraits, identical lantern charms, heavy Victorian costumes, written shop signs, busy street scenes, and nighttime effects that hide the face.

## Companion briefs

### Crumpet — Common

- **ID:** `lantern-lane-cats:crumpet`
- **Identity:** Round cream tabby; caramel stripes; teal scarf; friendly dark eyes.
- **Personality:** Warm, playful, and happiest when everyone has a comfortable place to begin.
- **Classic scene:** A bright café window with broad panes and a tiny steaming cup charm.
- **Sticker direction:** Late-afternoon window seat, plush cream fur, restrained café details, and a warm foreground glow without food clutter.

### Tansy — Common

- **ID:** `lantern-lane-cats:tansy`
- **Identity:** Bright tortoiseshell; green collar; asymmetrical coral and charcoal face patches; flower sprig.
- **Personality:** Cheerful and encouraging, always noticing where a little color would help.
- **Classic scene:** Pale garden light with one readable coral flower.
- **Sticker direction:** Florist doorway or flower-box setting with one clear sprig; avoid flower crowns and crowded bouquets.

### Puddle — Common

- **ID:** `lantern-lane-cats:puddle`
- **Identity:** Blue-gray coat; white muzzle and chest; yellow rain collar; blue drop charm.
- **Personality:** Curious, adventurous, and delighted by reflections and small discoveries after rain.
- **Classic scene:** Soft rain streaks and one broad puddle reflection.
- **Sticker direction:** Rain-washed lane at blue hour, one lifted paw near a reflection, face brightly readable, no full costume required.

### Wick — Common

- **ID:** `lantern-lane-cats:wick`
- **Identity:** Small charcoal kitten; oversized amber eyes; compact ears; tiny glowing lantern.
- **Personality:** Inventive, earnest, and brave about responsibilities that still feel a little big.
- **Classic scene:** Warm center glow against a simple violet dusk.
- **Sticker direction:** Lamplight on the kitten's cheeks near one low post or window; preserve the intentionally small silhouette.

### Inkwell — Uncommon

- **ID:** `lantern-lane-cats:inkwell`
- **Identity:** Neat tuxedo coat; indigo eyes and spectacles; white chest; open bound book.
- **Personality:** Thoughtful and careful, with a fondness for repairing well-loved things.
- **Classic scene:** Quiet bookshop windows and a graphic open-book shape.
- **Sticker direction:** Bookbinding counter with thread or paper implied softly; no written pages or floating letters.

### Chime — Uncommon

- **ID:** `lantern-lane-cats:chime`
- **Identity:** Lilac-point coat; violet eyes; blue collar; small golden bell.
- **Personality:** Dreamy and observant, hearing patterns and gentle rhythms in ordinary moments.
- **Classic scene:** Two restrained hanging chimes framing a pale face.
- **Sticker direction:** Clock-shop doorway with soft brass shapes and a slight breeze; avoid a wall full of clocks or music notation.

### Maribel — Uncommon

- **ID:** `lantern-lane-cats:maribel`
- **Identity:** Cinnamon longhair; broad cheek fluff; plum bow; gold stage-star charm.
- **Personality:** Gracious and welcoming with just enough theatrical flair to make effort feel special.
- **Classic scene:** Plum curtains and a bright central entrance.
- **Sticker direction:** Theater vestibule or curtain edge, poised welcoming gesture, no text, tickets, masks, or costume overload.

### Gable — Rare

- **ID:** `lantern-lane-cats:gable`
- **Identity:** Broad russet Maine Coon; ear tufts; green scarf; copper weather-vane charm.
- **Personality:** Adventurous, steady, and excellent at finding safe paths through complicated places.
- **Classic scene:** Layered roof silhouettes and a simple weather vane.
- **Sticker direction:** Confident rooftop stance in a golden breeze, strong scarf curve, face brighter than the skyline.

### Mosaic — Rare

- **ID:** `lantern-lane-cats:mosaic`
- **Identity:** Silver coat; graphic blue-gray spots; teal eyes; stained-glass diamond collar piece.
- **Personality:** Inventive and dreamy, always turning familiar things toward a new light.
- **Classic scene:** Three broad colored panes and a jewel assembled from simple color blocks.
- **Sticker direction:** Stained-glass workshop with colored light across the coat; keep effects geometric and restrained rather than rainbow-heavy.

### Lumina — Legendary

- **ID:** `lantern-lane-cats:lumina`
- **Identity:** Regal white longhair; gold ear accents; amber eyes; large lantern medallion.
- **Personality:** Warm, calm, and quietly radiant—the neighbor who makes sure everyone finds the way home.
- **Classic scene:** Three glowing lanterns forming an arch above the widest, brightest silhouette in the collection.
- **Sticker direction:** Lantern Night procession in a deep blue lane, luminous white-and-gold fur, controlled halo, and no crown or royal costume.

## Classic SVG acceptance

The first Classic pass is implemented in `public/collectibles/`. Review all ten together in the Art Lab at `/?dev=art` and in the playable collection before accepting Sticker prompts.

Acceptance checks:

- every face, coat, and signature remains distinct at 96 pixels;
- the four Commons are as appealing as higher rarities;
- grayscale silhouettes preserve Wick's small scale, Maribel and Lumina's longhair edges, Gable's broad ear tufts, and Chime's point markings;
- lantern and window effects do not flatten into one repeated yellow-purple palette;
- no SVG contains text or details that disappear at phone size;
- Sticker mode falls back to the same Classic asset without a broken image or ownership change.
