# How Number Nook Works

This is the plain-language guide to the game as it currently works. Planned changes belong in the topic documents and should appear here only after they are implemented.

## Practice rounds

Choose one or more operations, a difficulty, and a round length of 10, 20, 30, or 50 questions. Number Nook remembers those settings for the next round.

Each question has four choices. Correct answers add to accuracy, score, Paw Coins, progress, and skill history. Incorrect answers never remove progress or currency. At the end of a round, the results screen shows accuracy, thinking time, score, and Paw Coins; **Review questions** shows every problem and the selected and correct answers.

## Quick Game, Practice, and Trail Quest

Quick Game accepts one answer per question and measures accuracy and pace. Practice lets you take your time: wrong choices become disabled, a hint is available after the first miss, and help appears automatically after the second. Missed questions return in a short recap at the end. Only first answers earn score and coins; Practice has no speed bonus. Review shows attempts and hints as well as the first answer. See [Practice Mode](./PRACTICE_MODE.md).

Trail Quest places the equipped companion on a visual path. Every correct answer collects a unique treasure and advances one stop toward the picnic nook. A wrong answer records a detour and serves a fresh problem without moving the companion. The trail ends after ten correct answers, and results measure accuracy across every problem attempted. See [Trail Quest](./TRAIL_QUEST.md).

## Difficulty

- **Easy:** small numbers and introductory facts.
- **Medium:** broader elementary facts and larger addition and subtraction.
- **Hard:** substantially larger arithmetic, carrying and borrowing, and multiplication and division through 12.
- **Advanced:** the widest number ranges, negative subtraction answers, and deliberately demanding composition.

Difficulty changes both the questions and the Paw Coin reward for correct answers. Easy remains a valid and rewarding choice, while successful higher-difficulty practice earns a modest bonus for the additional time and effort.

## Score

Score measures correct and quick answering. It does not affect Paw Coins or unlocks.

- Every correct answer earns 100 points.
- A correct answer also earns up to 50 speed points.
- The speed bonus gradually falls until 10 seconds; a correct answer always earns at least 100 points.
- An incorrect answer earns no score for that question.

Score is useful for comparing similar rounds and personal improvement. Accuracy remains more important than rushing.

## Paw Coins

Paw Coins unlock companions and cannot be bought with real money.

The current round reward is calculated in this order:

1. Award 2 base coins for every correct answer.
2. Apply the difficulty multiplier and round to the nearest coin: Easy 1.00x, Medium 1.15x, Hard 1.30x, or Advanced 1.50x.
3. Add 2 coins for at least 80% accuracy.
4. Add 3 more coins for a perfect round.

A perfect ten-question round earns 25 Easy coins, 28 Medium coins, 31 Hard coins, or 35 Advanced coins. The results screen includes an expandable explanation of the award.

The first qualifying round each local day adds 5 coins; qualifying means at least five correct answers. Practicing on three different local days in one Monday-through-Sunday week adds another 15 coins. There is no daily earning cap. Reaching 100 coins earned in one day is a celebration milestone, and normal earnings continue afterward.

## Companions and capsules

Every player chooses a free starter companion. The first completed round unlocks one free Welcome Capsule. Additional companions come from the Capsule Shelf:

- a 60-coin Surprise Capsule can contain any eligible unowned companion;
- an 80-coin Collection Capsule contains an eligible unowned companion from the selected ordinary collection;
- every capsule awards an unowned eligible companion, so duplicates cannot appear;
- rarity weights affect which companion appears;
- a newly revealed companion can be equipped immediately;
- an equipped companion changes the game's colors and appears throughout the Nook;
- Classic SVG and Sticker art can be changed in Settings.

The collection gallery shows owned and undiscovered companions, collection progress, rarity, descriptions, and the equipped companion.

## Progress and history

Number Nook stores progress on the current device. It tracks scores, accuracy, timing, operations, difficulties, individual questions, capsules, companions, and longer-term aggregates.

The newest 30 rounds keep full question-level detail. Older rounds roll into compact lifetime statistics so history does not grow forever. Play history can be copied or exported for analysis, and a complete backup can be downloaded and restored on another device.

## Sound, music, and offline play

Sound effects and music have separate volume and mute controls in Settings. The game remains fully playable while muted.

Once installed or visited online, the PWA is designed to start and play offline. Progress remains local to the device unless a backup is exported and restored elsewhere.
